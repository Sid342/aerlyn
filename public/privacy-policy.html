Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 June 2026

1. Introduction

Home Decor ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Aerlyn smart-home app, which lets you control and automate compatible BLE and Wi-Fi smart switches in your home. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. It covers the Aerlyn mobile app as well as these supporting web pages. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to make Aerlyn work for you:

  • Account information you provide, such as your name and email address.
  • Home and room configuration you create, including room names, layouts, and the smart switches you add.
  • Device-control commands you issue, such as turning switches on or off and running scenes or schedules.
  • BLE and Wi-Fi device connectivity data needed to discover, pair, and communicate with your switches.
  • App usage and diagnostics, such as feature usage and crash or error logs, to help us keep the app stable.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to operate device control and automation, sync your home configuration across your sessions and devices, improve and develop app features, provide customer support, and maintain the security and integrity of the service.

4. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your data. We share information only with cloud hosting providers that store and process data on our behalf, and with voice assistant platforms such as Amazon Alexa and Google Home — but only when you choose to link those accounts, and only to the extent needed to enable voice control of your devices.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account remains active. If you ask us to delete your account or data, we will delete it from our active systems within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

6. Security

We protect your data using encryption in transit and access controls that limit who can reach it. While we work hard to safeguard your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, and delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us by email at the address below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

Aerlyn is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date above. Your continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
